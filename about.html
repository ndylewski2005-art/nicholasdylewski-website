About me

Driven by ideas, grounded in integrity

I’m a business student at the University of Miami Herbert Business School, co-founder and co-owner of Artemin.ai, and a future law student.

My vision

Where I’m headed

In a few years, I see myself in law school while continuing to explore entrepreneurship. I’m still open about which area of law I want to pursue, but I know I want a career that challenges me and gives me room to grow.

What ties it all together is simple: I care about helping people solve problems and turning good ideas into something useful.

What drives me

I like taking an idea and working at it until I can see the vision come to life. I get really invested in figuring out the steps, solving problems along the way, and watching all the pieces come together. That’s a big reason I’m drawn to entrepreneurship, and I think that same drive will carry into my career in law.
